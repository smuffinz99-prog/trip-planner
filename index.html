Master Checklist

Trip prep: paperwork, flights, transport, phone and money.

📋 Paperwork & Visas

  • Passports valid 6+ months beyond Oct 29
  • Flight confirmations saved offline (PDF on phone)
  • Hotel/hostel confirmations saved offline
  • Photocopies of passport photo page (paper)
  • Scanned passport emailed to yourself
  • Emergency contacts written on paper
  • Notify bank(s) of travel dates & countries
  • Get Wise / Revolut multi-currency card
  • Register Visit Japan Web (QR codes ready)
  • Malaysia MyTravelPass / MDAC submitted
  • Proof of accommodation printed/saved
  • Return flight booked & proof on hand
  • Notify employer / approve leave
  • Set email out-of-office auto-reply
  • Pay all bills due during the trip
  • Download Google Maps offline: KL, Tokyo, Osaka
  • Download Google Translate offline: Japanese + Malay
  • Register on SmartTraveller (smartraveller.gov.au)
  • Share full itinerary with someone at home
  • Prescription meds — full 30-day supply + scripts

✈️ Flights

  • All 4 legs booked & paid
  • Names match passports exactly
  • Overhead bag ≤ 7kg (tightest limit across all legs) Weigh it packed before you leave home
  • Backpack as personal item (under seat) + travel coat worn All three fly free — no checked bag fees
  • Oct 1: BNE→SYD (JQ829) lands 3–4h before Scoot departs Separate tickets = no protection if you miss it
  • Know your SYD T2/T3 → T1 transfer (2-min train / free T-Bus)
  • Web check-in done 48h before each flight
  • Boarding passes saved offline

🚉 Transport

  • Touch'n'Go card loaded on arrival KL
  • Grab app installed + payment added
  • ETS train KL→Penang booked (KTMB site)
  • Langkawi ferry/flight times checked
  • ICOCA/Suica added to phone wallet (or buy at KIX)
  • Kyoto→Tokyo night bus booked (Willer, Oct 23)
  • Tokyo→Narita N'EX time planned for Oct 29
  • Nankai KIX→Namba arrival transfer noted

📶 Phone & SIM

  • Aussie plan paused / set to cheapest
  • Number kept active for bank 2FA
  • Phone unlocked for foreign SIMs
  • eSIM compatibility checked
  • All essential apps installed
  • Offline maps + translate downloaded
  • Power bank charged (carry-on, not coat pocket at security)
  • Universal travel adapter packed

💳 Money

  • Wise card ordered & loaded with AUD
  • 2nd backup card packed separately
  • Bank notified of travel dates
  • $100 AUD emergency cash in bag
  • Always pay in LOCAL currency at terminals (not AUD)
  • All home bills auto-paid before leaving
